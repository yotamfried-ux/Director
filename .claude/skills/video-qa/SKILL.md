# Video QA

Follow `docs/05-qa.md`. Minimum before sending: contact sheet viewed, ffprobe matches the spec, captions readable and correct Hebrew, no unverified claim, demo data labelled. Tell Yotam what you could not check (audio, beat sync, WhatsApp behaviour, live site). If a defect shows (clipped text, ghost overlap, empty half-screen) fix and re-render rather than shipping with a note.
