# Creative brief — AI Agent System — site clip (v1, 2026-11-26)
- **Business goal (one):** a visitor of the portfolio understands that Yotam builds agent systems that must prove their work, and opens the project or contacts him.
- **Viewer / where / sound:** business owner or technical buyer, portfolio card (~630 px) and project page, no sound, a few seconds of attention.
- **What they believe or fear now:** AI agents are hype; an agent says "done" without proof, or acts without control.
- **The one promise (one sentence):** an AI agent team that has to prove its work, not just report it.
- **Desired action / next step:** open the project page / contact Yotam.
- **Claims verified on 2026-11-26 against the n8n spec workflow (last updated the same day):** V1-V11 in products/agent-system-bench.md; ranked proof: V5 evidence over claims, V4 failure loop, V2 separate roles (investigator vs verifier), V1 one run, V9/V7/V11 control.
- **Not shipped / not to claim (as of the same date):** any outcome number, proven self-improvement, production readiness, client availability. Kept out of the video; told to Yotam in the report.
- **Objections and the scene that answers each:** "does it just say it's done?" scene 5 (evidence order) and hook; "is it a black box?" scene 4 loop and run report chip; "will it act on its own?" outro chips (only on request, stops for approval); "does it improve itself?" outro chip (sandbox, separate check) with no result claimed.
- **Angles:** A Trust by evidence: promise "proof before claim", opening "I'm done. Who proves it?", proof order run, team, loop, evidence, control; risk: abstract. B The team: promise "fifteen specialists on one request", opening the crew grid; risk: feature list. C Control: promise "it only does what you ask and stops for approval", opening the boundary; risk: sounds defensive. **Recommended: A**, with B's roles in one scene and C's lines in the outro, because the viewer's fear is unchecked claims and the spec supports it best; no outcome numbers exist.
- **Form:** 30 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, Hebrew RTL, silent, six scenes with 24-frame blur fades, one slow continuous drift per scene so nothing freezes; reading budget 0.4 s per word plus hold.
- **Success looks like:** qualitative: the 5-second test (hook plus name by 5 s); no analytics, because the benchmark is not published.
- **Open questions for Yotam (max 3, with recommendation):** (1) May "self-improvement" appear at all? Recommend yes, only as sandbox plus separate check, as drawn. (2) Add an English variant? Recommend later. (3) Keep the benchmark unpublished until a real result exists. 
## Director's review
- 5-second test: pass. The hook ("סיימתי" / "ומי מוכיח?") is up at frame 1 and the name "AI Agent System" appears at 4.0 s; the promise (proof before claim) is repeatable by the end of scene 5.
- Message: one promise, no outcome numbers. Honesty: every line maps to V1-V11; the self-improvement chip states process only. Nothing negative about status appears.
- Feel: soft 24-frame blur fades, no hard cuts (qa-gate: 0 cuts), no frozen tail (first render failed on a 1.6 s static tail; fixed with continuous linear drift in the loop, hook and outro). qa-gate still warns about a 3.4 s quiet hold in the hook (0.7-4.1 s) with only slow drift: deliberate, it gives the question time to land.
- Cut test: the evidence scene could be shorter; kept because it carries the promise.
- Not verified: motion feel in real playback, readability at the real ~630 px card size, an English variant, Yotam's view of the wording.
