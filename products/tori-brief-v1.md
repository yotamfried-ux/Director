# Creative brief: Tori reels for 2026-11-29 (v1)
- **Business goal:** an owner of an appointment business taps the profile link (turim.d2ryf.com) and tries Tori.
- **Viewer and moment:** Israeli small-business owner scrolling Instagram/Facebook at commute, lunch or evening; sound probably off; 2 seconds of attention.
- **One promise per reel:** p1 your customers book alone, no app, no password. p2 your whole day is visible at a glance. p3 reminders and a waitlist work for you.
- **Desired action:** open the link in bio.
- **Proof (all in products/tori.md "Verified claims", re-verified 2026-11-28):** public booking page, free slots only, instant confirmation; owner calendar with today's KPIs; day-before reminder with one-click arrival confirmation; waitlist offer to the first waiting customer.
- **Objections:** "is it hard for my customers?" answered by p1 (four steps); "will I see everything?" p2; "what about cancellations?" p3. Not answered: price, payments (forbidden).

## Angles (named A/B/C)
- A: three distinct pillars (customer / owner / automation). Recommended: different hook and screen each, no repeated footage.
- B: one flow, three hooks (clean test, repeated footage). Rejected: repetition.
- C: one long plus two loops. Rejected: breaks the three-reels decision.
Chosen: A. Form: 15 s, 9:16, Hebrew captions, hook <2 s; the length gives each idea one beat per ~3 s.
