# Marketing method: think like the buyer

Yotam's instruction: "put yourself in the shoes of an independent business owner; think what you'd look for; go beyond basic features; think marketing."

## Buyer lens (small appointment-based businesses: barbers, beauty, therapists, coaches, teachers)
| Pain | What the owner wants | Tori proof (verified) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone/WhatsApp ping-pong "when are you free?" | Customers book alone | Public booking link, no account/app/password, only free slots shown |
| No-shows, last-minute cancels, empty slots | Fewer empty hours | Auto reminders with confirm-arrival link; waitlist offers a cancelled slot to the first waiting customer; no-show policy (off/warn/approval/block) |
| Loss of control | Own the rules | Cancel/reschedule window, manual approval for new bookings, no-show handling |
| "Is the business doing well?" | Numbers, not feelings | Reports: appointments, revenue (completed visits), no-show & cancel rate, new vs returning, by day/service/weekday; CSV/Excel export |
| Team / admin overhead | One calendar | Day/week/month calendar, staff with own hours & services, exceptions/vacations, customers folder |

## Message hierarchy (Tori)
Hook: **"פחות הודעות. יותר תורים."** → customer books alone → it's already in the calendar → it runs itself (reminder, waitlist, your rules) → you see what's happening (reports) → logo.

## Copy rules (from Engineering-OS `human-centered-ux-writing`)
Specific not grandiose · say what happens next · everyday Hebrew, short sentences · no invented urgency, guarantees or testimonials · numerals tabular · RTL reviewed by a human.

## Evidence discipline
Every claim → a source line in `products/<slug>.md`. If it is not verified, it is not in the video. Synthetic buyer research (e.g. Engineering-OS `founder-skill`) gives hypotheses only, never proof.
