# lead-followup (event)
Follow `routines/agents/_common.md`. Use `lead-manager`.
Input: one lead key from D2R (or Yotam's message).
1. Classify the lead, check consent and opt-out first. An opted-out lead gets no draft.
2. Draft a short Hebrew reply and a next action with a due date. Do not send.
3. Report without personal data; the draft goes to Yotam in chat only.
Consent none and not opted out: a short service-only reply to the person's own request is allowed as a draft for Yotam; no promotion, no marketing follow-up, at most 2 reminders, each with a due date. Opted out: no draft at all. No lead key given: nothing to do; say "blocked on D2R" if the store is not connected. Reports carry the lead key only, never name or contact.
